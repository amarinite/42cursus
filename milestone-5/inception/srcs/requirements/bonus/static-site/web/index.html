Not Just a "Thief"

The Eurasian Magpie (Pica pica) is widely considered one of the most intelligent animals in the world. Often unfairly labeled as mere "shiny object thieves," these birds possess complex social structures and problem-solving skills that rival Great Apes.

The Mirror Test

The magpie is one of the few non-mammal species to pass the Mirror Self-Recognition (MSR) test. When a colored sticker is placed on their neck—a spot they can only see in a reflection—they will attempt to scratch it off, proving they understand the reflection is themselves and not another bird.

Folklore vs. Reality

Myth Reality
They steal silver. Studies show they actually fear new, shiny objects (Neophobia).
They are omens of bad luck. In many cultures, like Korea, they are seen as harbingers of good news.
